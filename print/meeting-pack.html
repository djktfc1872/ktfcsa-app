Kettering Town FC Supporters' Association

What this is, and what we are asking for

We are supporters of Kettering Town. In August we asked the fanbase what it thought about how the club is run. 199 people answered in five days. This is what they said and what we have done about it.

What you told us

151 of 199 say the club is going in the wrong direction. Nine say it is going the right way.
92% are worried about volunteers leaving. It is the biggest concern of the lot.
84% named the team on the pitch as the thing they are proudest of. This is not about the football.
83% say the ownership does not represent them.

What we asked the club

Twelve questions in writing on 22 August, ten of them written by supporters, with a reply asked for by 4 September.

The club replied on 3 September, inside the date, and we have published their letter in full. It does not answer the twelve one by one. It does commit to meeting the Supporters' Trust more often, to fixing the website and social media, and to monthly updates or a fans forum. We intend to hold them to those.

What we want to happen

  1. Explain the volunteer departures, and how volunteers are recruited and kept.
  2. Answer the twelve questions in writing, and publish the answers.
  3. Name everyone on the board and any committee, with their role and start date.
  4. Meet a recognised supporters' body regularly, on a set date, on the record.
  5. Set out which company holds the ground, the lease and the debts.
  6. Publish a financial report a supporter can actually read.

What about the Trust?

The Poppies Supporters Trust already exists and the club points to it. 35% of you feel it represents you well, 34% feel it does not. We are not competing with them and we will talk to them first. The difference is simple: they are a membership body with a fee, and we think being a Kettering supporter should be enough to be counted.

Tonight, if people are willing

  • Agree the Association exists, and elect an interim committee from the floor.
  • Name someone to write to the club on our behalf.
  • A two page constitution inside a month, published for everyone.
  • A proper election, open to every member, within three months.

Getting involved

Everything so far has been done by supporters in their own time. It is free, there are no adverts and nothing is behind a paywall. Put your name on the sheet going round, or come and find one of us.
